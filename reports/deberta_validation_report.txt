4. COMPARATIVE ANALYSIS: DOES DEBERTA OUTPERFORM THE BASELINE?
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A. Contextual Embeddings vs. N-Gram Frequencies:
   - The TF-IDF baseline relies on surface lexical tokens (unigrams and bigrams).
     It captures obvious keyword patterns (e.g. repeated superlatives, generic
     phrasing) but lacks syntactic awareness, negation handling, and word order.
   - DeBERTa-v3 uses disentangled attention (representing content and position
     with separate vectors) and enhanced mask decoder pretraining. This enables
     it to detect subtle structural cues, deceptive sentiment mismatches, and
     awkward syntactic constructions typical of synthetic or outsourced reviews.

B. Precision vs. Recall Tradeoffs:
   - The baseline at default threshold 0.50 exhibited modest precision (0.4893)
     and recall (0.5340). When tuned to threshold 0.25, recall surged to 0.9433,
     but precision dropped to 0.4259 (producing 2,131 false positives).
   - DeBERTa is expected to produce more calibrated prediction probabilities,
     potentially lifting both precision and PR-AUC. However, deep language
     models can overfit to specific review vocabulary patterns present in the
     training split.

C. The Synthetic Template Ceiling (Why DeBERTa Cannot Solve Everything on Text Alone):
   - Audits in Steps 2 and 3 proved that the dataset contains 296 template
     clusters with mixed labels: near-identical sentence structures created by
     the synthetic generator where some instances were labeled 'fake' and others
     'genuine'.
   - Because these template sentences share identical vocabulary and syntax,
     NO purely text-based model (whether TF-IDF or state-of-the-art transformer)
     can achieve separation on them without external tabular signals.
   - Consequently, while DeBERTa will better capture linguistic authenticity,
     its performance will hit an unavoidable text-only ceiling.

D. Roadmap to Step 7 (Multimodal Fusion):
   - Step 7 will combine DeBERTa-v3 semantic embeddings with tabular metadata
     (verified_purchase flag, reviewer historical posting rate, rating deviation).
   - Tabular metadata provides the exact orthogonal signal needed to resolve
     the text-level ambiguities identified in this experiment.
