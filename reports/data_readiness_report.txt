4. REVIEW QUALITY AUDIT & LINGUISTIC ARTIFACTS
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A deep qualitative inspection of review texts reveals clear evidence of synthetic
template-based generation. Removing exact duplicates does NOT make the text
wholly natural. Key linguistic characteristics observed:

A. Repetitive Structural Templates:
   - Common Opening Templates:
     * 'I spent time using this [product] in different situations...' (938 occurrences)
     * 'I expected more from this [product] given the...' (651 occurrences)
     * 'I ended up using this [product] more than I initially...' (595 occurrences)
     * 'I was mostly interested in how the [attribute] would hold up...' (589 occurrences)
   - Common Closing Templates:
     * '...deciding how I felt about it.' (933 occurrences)
     * '...more to me than the appearance.' (669 occurrences)
     * '...ordinary use without any serious problems.' (371 occurrences)

B. Slot-Filling Glitches:
   - Generic templates are populated with product-specific nouns without adapting
     grammatical number, creating noticeable agreement errors:
     * 'the controls is convenient without being overcomplicated' (plural subject, singular verb)
     * 'the buttons is convenient without being overcomplicated'
     * 'the examples is weaker than I expected'

C. Near-Duplicate Review Clusters (Threshold >= 0.90):
   - Total Near-Duplicate Pairs  : 6,082
   - Total Reviews Involved      : 3,536 (11.86% of dataset)
   - Total Multi-Review Clusters : 1,189
   - Cluster Purity Breakdown:
     * Pure Fake Clusters        : 348 clusters (1,343 reviews)
     * Pure Genuine Clusters     : 545 clusters (1,188 reviews)
     * Mixed-Label Clusters      : 296 clusters (1,005 reviews)

D. Critical Implication of Mixed-Label Clusters:
   In 296 clusters, virtually identical template sentences (similarity >= 0.90)
   are assigned conflicting labels ('fake' vs 'genuine'). For example:
   Cluster CLUST_0024:
     [RV-WUY4SP] (GENUINE): 'My first impression was the padding is neither a major strength nor a deal breaker. I used it during a normal weekday rather than just testing it once.'
     [RV-DEFQW3] (FAKE)   : 'My first impression was the blade is neither a major strength nor a deal breaker. I used it during a normal weekday rather than just testing it once.'
   This proves the synthetic generation process reused template scaffolds across
   both classes. Random train/test splitting could cause data leakage if near-identical
   templates appear in both train and test splits. Cluster-aware or stratified
   splitting is strongly recommended.
