4. IN-DEPTH ERROR ANALYSIS & ROOT CAUSES
A. Root Cause of False Negatives (FN = 1,206 at 0.50):
1. High-Confidence Genuine Bias: Because the training set is 62.5% genuine and unweighted cross-entropy
   was used, the model predicts low fake probabilities (mean 0.231 for FN) on ambiguous text.
2. Subtle Synthetic Phrasing: Many synthetic fake reviews mimic normal positive or neutral language
   without blatant scam keywords.
3. Pure Text Limitation: A review like 'I received this product and the quality was satisfactory' cannot
   be determined as fake by language alone without checking if the reviewer is verified or posting abnormally.

B. Impact of Threshold Tuning:
Lowering the decision threshold from 0.50 to 0.12 (derived strictly from validation data)
dramatically recovers missed fake reviews, raising test recall from 28.1% to 92.2%
and lifting test F1-score from 0.4243 to 0.6019.
