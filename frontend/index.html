Diagnostic Analysis & Observations

Step 5 Report

Leakage-Protected Benchmark: The baseline achieves an ROC-AUC of 0.6691 and PR-AUC of 0.6118. Because data splits strictly isolated reviewer IDs and template clusters, the model cannot cheat by memorizing author styles.

Threshold Tradeoff: At threshold 0.50, F1 is 0.5107 with balanced precision and recall. Tuning to threshold 0.25 surges recall to 0.9433 (catching 94% of fake reviews), but precision drops to 0.4259 with 2,131 false alarms.

Synthetic Template Ceiling: Audits revealed 296 template clusters with mixed labels (near-identical phrases labeled both fake and genuine). Purely text-based bag-of-words cannot separate these without external tabular signals.

How to Sync Colab Checkpoints into Backend (Requirement 9)

Step-by-Step Walkthrough

Once your DeBERTa-v3 training notebook (training/notebooks/train_deberta.ipynb) finishes in Google Colab, copy the saved files into the local artifacts folder:

fake-review-detection-v2/training/artifacts/deberta_text/
├── config.json
├── model.safetensors (or pytorch_model.bin)
├── tokenizer.json
├── tokenizer_config.json
├── special_tokens_map.json
└── validation_metrics.json

The backend ModelService is already programmed to detect these files on startup or reload. Once placed, restart the backend server or switch MODEL_TYPE=deberta_text in .env to immediately serve live transformer inferences!
