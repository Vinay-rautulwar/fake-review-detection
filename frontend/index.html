Scientific Transparency: Training Loss Curves Policy

Step-by-step training and validation loss logs were not persisted to disk in historical runs of DeBERTa-v3 or BiLSTM prior to final checkpoint exportation. In accordance with rigorous scientific integrity guidelines, training loss curves are explicitly omitted rather than simulated with pseudo-random or fabricated mathematical functions.
